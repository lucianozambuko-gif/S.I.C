Process

How the work actually happens.

Attention gets someone to watch once. Retention is what turns that into a fan base, organic traffic and subscribers who come back without being chased.

1

Hook for attention

First frame, first words, first cut, built to earn a stop in a feed that owes you nothing.

2

Pace for retention

Edits paced to hold watch time and answer the question the hook opened, so people stay to the end.

3

Post for growth

A set cadence and clear next step turn one-time viewers into followers, and followers into subscribers.

How we grow a brand

For brands, the process starts with the audience, not the product. We study who's already engaging with you, build content in their language and on their platforms, and keep the visual identity consistent across every campaign. Growth that resonates doesn't come from shouting louder. It comes from showing up as something the audience already wants to follow.
